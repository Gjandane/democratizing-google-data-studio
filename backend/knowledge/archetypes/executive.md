# Decision overview

Lead with a small set of headline KPIs, then trends and drivers. Ask who decides, which targets matter, and how often decisions are made. Prioritize the high-priority business questions over detail. Use scorecards, time_series and ranked bars; avoid dense tables above the fold.

Use the shared 12-column grid and a single page. Choose only allowed catalog components and connect each high-priority question to a question_ref. Confirm metric units, aggregation and time grain before planning.
