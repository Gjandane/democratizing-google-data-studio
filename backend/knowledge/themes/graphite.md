# Graphite

Default for analytical reports. Slate headings and a precise horizontal rule
give tables and comparisons the same stable frame. Compact density leaves
room for detail; a small radius keeps adjacent chart surfaces easy to scan.
Title 28px bold, subtitle 18px, labels 14px; left aligned. Series remain
categorical, never a misleading continuous gradient.
