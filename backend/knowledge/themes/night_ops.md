# Night Ops

Default for operational reports. A legible navy canvas and roomy letterforms
serve a monitoring desk. The short cyan rule anchors the header; grouping
panels distinguish the current signal from its breakdown, without glows or
status colors that imply thresholds the data has not supplied.
Title 28px bold, subtitle 18px, labels 14px; left aligned on the shared grid.
